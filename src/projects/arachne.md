Arachne is the working name for an ongoing thread of experiments and
notes that do not fit cleanly inside a single writing or document. It
has its own section because the entries refer to one another more than
they refer to anything else.

See [/projects/arachne/](/projects/arachne/) for the thread itself.

## Status

Active. Updated when there is something to record and not before.

## Conventions

Each Arachne entry is numbered (`A-001`, `A-002`, ...) in addition to
being dated. Numbers are stable; dates are not always strictly
increasing, because earlier numbers may be revised in place.

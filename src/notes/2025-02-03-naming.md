---
title: Naming things twice
date: 2025-02-03
summary: A working rule for filenames and identifiers.
---

When in doubt, name a thing twice: once for the machine, once for the
human. The machine wants `2025-02-03-naming.md`. The human wants
"Naming things twice." Neither name should try to do both jobs.

When the names disagree later, that disagreement is information.

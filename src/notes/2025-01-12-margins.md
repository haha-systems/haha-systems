---
title: On margins
date: 2025-01-12
summary: A note on why empty space is load-bearing.
---

The cheapest way to make a page feel considered is to leave more of it
empty than feels comfortable. The page should look slightly under-filled
on a wide monitor; that is the correct amount.

A document with no margin reads as urgent. A document with too much
reads as precious. The middle band is narrower than people expect.

---
title: Why "haha"
date: 2025-03-19
summary: On taking a name that does not perform seriousness.
---

The name does not have to do any work. Naming a workshop after a sound
of involuntary laughter buys a small amount of distance from the genres
of "studio," "labs," and "research." The work can then be as serious as
it needs to be without the title trying to prove it.
